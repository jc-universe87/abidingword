# Verify a claim

Record (a) exact claim, (b) scope and time sensitivity, (c) authoritative sources, (d) direct evidence with retrieval date, (e) conflicts, (f) verdict: VERIFIED, CONTRADICTED, PARTIALLY SUPPORTED or NOT VERIFIED. For dynamic provider/model data, never extrapolate pricing or support from an obsolete list. Never treat a link, a model answer or search snippet as proof of live behaviour.
