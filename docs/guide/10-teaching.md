# 🎤 10. Preparing to teach

[← Questions and word list](09-questions-and-word-list.md) · [Contents](README.md)

---

When your study is ready to be shared, Abiding Word helps you **prepare the
teaching itself**: a sermon, a small-group Bible study, a lesson for young
people or children, a talk. **You write it.** The program keeps your study
close at hand, and, only if you want, an **assistant** can give you a second
pair of eyes.

## Starting

1. Open your study.
2. Under the study's passages, click **Teaching** (click **Study** to go back).
3. Choose what you are preparing: *Sermon*, *Group Bible study*, *Youth*,
   *Children*, *Lesson*, *Talk* or *Other*.
4. Change the title if you like, and click **Start**.

Nothing is filled in for you. Every part may stay empty until you need it.
You can prepare **more than one teaching** from the same study (for example
a Sunday sermon and a youth lesson): click **Prepare another teaching**, and
switch between them with **Teaching:**.

## The parts of a teaching

| Part | What to write |
|---|---|
| **Who it is for** | e.g. the whole church, a youth group, children aged 8–10 |
| **Purpose** | what you hope the teaching will do |
| **Central message** | in your own words, the one thing you most want people to take away |
| **Introduction** | how you begin |
| **Main points** | as many as you need, in any order and any shape: a part of a story, a question, a theme, a few verses. Each has a **title**, an **explanation**, an **application** and an **illustration or example** |
| **Conclusion** | how you finish |
| **Notes for yourself** | reminders while you teach; not part of what you say |

Everything is **saved as you type**. Move a point with the ▲ ▼ buttons
beside it; delete it with 🗑 (if you wrote in it, you are asked first).

## Using your study

- **Use a finding from the study** puts one of your study's findings under the
  whole teaching or under a point. It stays in your study: if you change it
  there, it changes here too, and **Stop using this finding here** (✕) never
  deletes it. A finding you made from a book still shows its quotation and
  opens the book at those words.
- **Add a passage** names a Bible passage for the whole teaching or for one
  point. Click it to open it in the Bible. It is not added to your study's
  own passages.

## The assistant (optional)

The assistant is **off** until you turn it on in **Settings → Assistant**
(see [Settings](08-settings-updates-backups.md#assistant)). When it is on,
each part of your teaching has a small **💡 Help** button.

</p>

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **You are the author.** The assistant never writes your teaching for you
> and never changes your text by itself. It suggests; you decide.

### What you can ask

| Group | Ask for… |
|---|---|
| **Wording** | Check grammar and spelling · Make it clearer · Make it shorter · Make it sound natural · Remove repetition · Easier to say aloud · Adapt for an audience · Translate · Help with a transition |
| **Comments** | Review what I wrote · Does the application follow from the passage? · Check theology and the passage · Suggest passages to look at |
| **Ideas** | Other ways to say this · Help with a discussion question · Ideas for an illustration · Find a real story · Suggest a visual |

Beside **Main points** you can also ask for a comment on the **structure** of
the whole teaching, or how you could **order** what you already have.

### How it works

1. Click **Help** beside a part and choose what you want.
   *Tip:* select a few words first to work on just those.
2. The assistant says **what will be shared and where it goes**, for example
   "To help, this will be sent to OpenAI: your Introduction; your central
   message; Scripture: John 15:1–8; 2 findings from your study". With a
   program on your own computer it says "This stays on this computer".
   **Nothing is sent until you click Ask** (or press Ctrl+Enter).
3. You see the answer **apart from your text**:
   - **Suggested wording** shows exactly what would change (struck-through
     words are removed, underlined words added). Your text stays as it is
     unless you click **Use this wording**, and **Undo** brings your earlier
     text back.
   - **Comments** say what they are based on: Scripture, your study, a book in
     your library, or *the assistant's own thought, to weigh for yourself*.
     A suggested passage is added to your teaching only if you click **Add**.
   - **Illustration ideas** are always marked **invented examples, not real
     events**. Do not tell them as if they happened.
4. Press **Escape** or click **Done** to close it. Nothing has changed unless
   you chose it.

If a part is still empty, the assistant does not fill it: *"Write something
here first. The assistant helps with what you have written."*

### Real stories

**Find a real story…** looks for true stories, events or examples. Describe
in a few words what you are looking for (e.g. *forgiveness after a conflict*).

- This only works with a **search service** set up in Settings. Without one,
  the assistant tells you it cannot check real stories; it does not make one
  up.
- Each story shows **where it came from** (click a source to open it in your
  browser) and how far it is checked:
  - *Reported by at least two independent sources the search found*, or
  - *Not checked: only one source. Please check before you use it.*
- Always read the sources yourself before you use a story.
- **Keep with this teaching** stores the story with its sources under the
  part you asked about.

### Pictures

**Suggest a visual** gives ideas for a photo, a simple diagram or a drawing.
For each idea you can:

- **Find freely licensed images** (from Wikimedia Commons): each shows its
  licence and author, and **Where it is from**.
- **Make an image**, if you turned this on in Settings: always marked
  *Made by the assistant: an illustration, not a photograph*.
- **Keep** an idea or an image. Nothing is placed in your teaching unless you
  keep it.

Kept stories, ideas and images appear under the part they belong to, with a
dashed border and their label, so they never look like your own writing.

## Your privacy

- The assistant is optional. Abiding Word works fully without it.
- It acts only when you click **Ask**, never while you type.
- It sees only the part you asked about and what it needs from **this**
  teaching and **this** study (listed before you ask), never your other
  studies, your whole library or your other teachings.
- Your key is kept in your computer's own password store, not in your
  studies.
