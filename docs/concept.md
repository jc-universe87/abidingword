## What Abiding Word is

A local-first, cross-platform **Bible Study Workspace** for pastors, teachers
and serious students of Scripture.

- **Brand:** Abiding Word
- **Descriptor:** Bible Study Workspace
- **Tagline:** Study with diligence. Teach with love.

## Core workflow

**Word → Understanding → Teaching**

The logomark carries the same movement: page edge (the Word), colon (study
and understanding), chevron (teaching and communication).

## Principles

- Scripture at the centre
- Local-first: a genuine desktop application that needs no server
- Offline-capable
- User-owned data, kept in one folder on the user's own computer
- Cross-platform: Windows, macOS and Linux; desktop first
- AI-assisted later, but never AI-dependent
- Interoperable with external systems later
- Simple enough for non-technical pastors
- Powerful enough for serious long-term Bible study

## Core objects

- **Workspace** — everything a user owns on this computer.
- **Project** — a group of related studies: a sermon series, a Bible study
  course, a doctrine.
- **Study** — the primary unit of work, usually one passage, e.g.
  Romans 8:1–17.
- **Study section** — a stage of the study, e.g. Text, Context,
  Observations, Structure, Exegesis, Theology, Application, Audience,
  Teaching. Sections come from templates and are not fixed forever.
- **Study block** — one piece of content within a section: paragraph, note,
  heading; later quotation, question, word study, source citation and more.

## Phases (as stated in the brief)

- **Foundation v0.1** — desktop shell, local data folder, database with
  migrations, projects, studies, sections and simple blocks, settings and
  theme. No AI, Scripture engine, source ingestion, sync or MCP.
- **Later** — Scripture engine, personal source library and import,
  concordance and original-language tools, optional AI assistance, sync,
  integrations, MCP.
