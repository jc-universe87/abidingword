## How Abiding Word uses it

The archive is shipped unmodified as an application resource. It is not
installed automatically; the user adds it from Settings → Bible Texts (or
the Scripture pane) through the normal import verification screen.

The importer keeps the words of every verse unchanged. It leaves out
non-text markup: Strong's number tags, footnotes, cross-references, book
titles and section headings. Psalm titles are kept. Because the wording is
not changed, the text keeps its name, as the licence asks.

Attribution shown in the app: "World English Bible — Public Domain.
"World English Bible" is a trademark of eBible.org."
