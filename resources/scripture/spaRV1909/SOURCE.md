## How Abiding Word uses it

The archive is shipped unmodified as an application resource. It is not
installed automatically; the user adds it from Settings → Bible Texts (or the
Scripture pane) through the normal import verification screen.

The importer keeps the words of every verse unchanged. It leaves out
non-text markup: footnotes, cross-references, book titles and section
headings. Psalm titles are kept.

Attribution shown in the app: "Reina Valera 1909 — dominio público (Public Domain)."
