## Licence statement

From `copr.htm` inside the archive (kept alongside it, unchanged):

> The Holy Bible in English: Berean Standard Bible. Public Domain.
> Language: English. Contributor: BSB Publishing, LLC.

The details page (https://ebible.org/find/details.php?id=engbsb) lists the
translation as "public domain", and eBible.org's catalogue marks it
redistributable and certified.

The translators' own site (berean.bible) could not be reached from the build
environment; the statement above is eBible.org's, the same standard used for
the World English Bible.

## How Abiding Word uses it

The archive is shipped unmodified as an application resource. It is not
installed automatically; the user adds it from Settings → Bible Texts (or the
Scripture pane) through the normal import verification screen.

The importer keeps the words of every verse unchanged. It leaves out
non-text markup: footnotes, cross-references, book titles and section
headings. Psalm titles are kept.

Attribution shown in the app: "Berean Standard Bible — Public Domain."
